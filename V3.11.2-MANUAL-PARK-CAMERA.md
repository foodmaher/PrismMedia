# Version 3.11.2: manual park camera

This release is based directly on the working 3.11.0 SPF traffic-radio build.
Its Integrated Media, Spotify audio, WebView helper and DirectInput cursor paths
are unchanged.

## Park-camera alignment

Select **Internal park camera**, enable **Preview / calibrate now**, then use the
Park camera alignment controls. Position, height, yaw and pitch are saved per
screen. The default anchor follows the truck without SPF. Enable **Follow final
connected trailer (SPF)** only when the camera should follow the last trailer.

The left/right and up/down flip buttons affect only the internal park-camera
image. They do not flip normal GPS media.

## Automatic brightness cadence

The game-lighting sampler now updates at half the selected GPS display refresh
rate. A 30 Hz GPS therefore samples lighting at 15 Hz while the screen continues
to present at 30 Hz. The asynchronous 4 x 4 sampler remains dormant when
automatic brightness is disabled.
