# Version 3.5: persistent environment audio mixer

## What changed

Version 3.5 removes the synthesized noise source. The plugin is silent until
at least one valid local audio file is configured. Open **Ctrl+F8 > Open-Window
Wind Audio** and add files to any of these independent layers:

- **Stationary sounds** for stopped or very-low-speed ambience such as birds;
- **City sounds** for low and medium road speed; and
- **Highway sounds** for sustained high-speed airflow.

Each layer accepts multiple WAV, MP3, WMA, M4A or AAC files. Files play in
order and the layer advances when the current file ends. The three layers stay
open in the media helper and speed changes crossfade their volumes. Closing a
window fades the result to silence without pausing, reopening or resetting the
audio files. Missing files and empty layers are ignored.

## Window and stereo behavior

Bind the four window controls to the same keys used by ETS2/ATS. The shared
volume follows truck speed and the estimated opening of both windows. The
**Open-side stereo separation** slider controls how strongly the sound moves
toward the open window. At 100%, a right-only opening is heard primarily from
the right channel; at 0%, the mix remains centered.

## Media reduction

**Media reduction at full wind** lowers integrated YouTube and Spotify audio as
the environment mix becomes louder. A setting of 100% makes the media silent
at full environment intensity and restores the original media level smoothly
as the windows close or the environment sound fades. This does not change the
game's own engine, traffic or UI audio.

## Suggested setup

- Use seamless loops of at least 30 seconds to hide the loop boundary.
- Keep stationary ambience quieter than city and highway airflow.
- Start highway transition around 50-60 km/h and finish around 85-100 km/h.
- Use 80-90% stereo separation for a clear side cue without hard-panning.
- Keep files in a permanent folder; moving them later makes that entry silent.
