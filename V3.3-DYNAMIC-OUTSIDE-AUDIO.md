# PrismTextureStreamerFB 3.3 Dynamic Outside Audio

## Closed-cab external sound

External cameras no longer begin with the unrestricted inside-cab sound.
Users can configure:

- outside-cab volume beside the truck;
- outside-cab cutoff beside the truck;
- minimum volume at the far distance;
- far-distance cutoff; and
- the full-volume and far-distance points.

Volume scales smoothly and cutoff scales logarithmically between the selected
near and far values. The cutoff range extends down to 20 Hz, but 20 Hz is not
forced.

## Spotify controls

The official Spotify Embed API does not provide next-track or previous-track
methods. For Spotify mode, the plugin's Next and Previous controls therefore
cycle through the user's saved Spotify links. YouTube playlist Next and
Previous remain unchanged.
